# 🎓 ScholarshipMN Academy
### **Connecting Mongolian Youth to Global Opportunities**

ScholarshipMN is a mission-driven platform created to bridge the information gap for Mongolian students seeking international and local education opportunities. By centralizing scholarship data, we empower the next generation of Mongolian talent to achieve their academic dreams.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission
Many talented Mongolian students miss out on life-changing opportunities because information is scattered, outdated, or hard to navigate. **ScholarshipMN** was built to solve this by:
* **Centralizing Access:** Bringing scattered scholarship data into one unified, real-time portal.
* **Simplifying the Search:** Providing intuitive tools to help students find scholarships that match their specific goals.
* **Empowering Students:** Giving every Mongolian youth an equal starting line to build a global career.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Curated Database:** A hand-picked selection of scholarships specifically relevant to Mongolian applicants.
* **Real-time Updates:** Powered by **Firebase**, the platform provides instant updates on new openings and deadlines.
* **Personalized Dashboards:** Secure user authentication (Phone/Email) allows students to save, track, and manage their applications.
* **High-Performance UI:** Built with **Next.js 14** for a seamless experience, even on slower mobile networks in rural areas.

## 🛠 Technical Stack
* **Framework:** [Next.js 14](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router & Server Components)
* **Backend:** [Firebase Firestore](https://firebase.google.com/) (Real-time NoSQL database)
* **Authentication:** [Firebase Auth](https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth) (Secure Phone/SMS & Email login)
* **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) (Modern, mobile-first design)
* **Analytics:** [Google Analytics (GA4)](https://analytics.google.com/) - Using data to understand and serve student needs better.

---

## 📊 Impact-First Approach
We don't just measure code; we measure impact. By using Firebase Analytics, we track engagement to see which fields of study are most in demand among Mongolian students, allowing us to continuously optimize our data feed to serve the community's needs.
